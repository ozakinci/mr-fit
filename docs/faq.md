# FAQ

> This describes the current build. See [how-to-play.md](how-to-play.md) for a note on the fitness rework in progress.

**Does my progress save?**
Yes, automatically, in your browser. You don't need to do anything.

**Why can't I afford anything?**
Keep clicking, or wait — currency builds up on its own once you own at least one generator. Buy buttons light up as soon as you can afford them.

**Why did the cost go up after I bought a generator?**
Each generator you own makes the next copy of that same generator more expensive. It keeps the game from being solved by buying one thing forever — spreading purchases around usually works better.

**What happens if I close the tab / turn off my computer?**
Nothing bad — your progress is saved. When you come back, you'll even get a bit of currency for the time you were away.

**Is there an ending?**
Not right now — it's an ongoing idle loop. Bigger long-term goals (like a prestige/reset system) may be added later; check the project's roadmap if you're curious what's planned.

**I think I found a bug. Where do I report it?**
Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository, if it's hosted publicly, or contact whoever gave you the game.

**Can I move my progress to a different browser or computer?**
Not yet — save data lives in your current browser only. Save export/import is on the roadmap.
