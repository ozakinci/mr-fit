# How to Play Mr. Fit

Mr. Fit is a bodyweight home workout game. No equipment, no gym — just you, your browser, and four kinds of exercise: Push, Pull, Legs, and Core.

## Quick start

1. Hit the big **Work Out!** button in the middle of the screen. Every exercise — Push, Pull, Legs, and Core — gets 1 rep, all from that one click.
2. Each exercise also has its own button, e.g. **Do a set (+10)** on Push. Click it to knock out a full set of just that exercise in one go, instead of clicking one rep at a time.
3. Every rep you log — from the big button or from a set — builds your **muscle**, shown at the top of the screen.
4. Keep working out. Muscle and reps only go up.

That's the whole loop: click, rep out, watch your numbers grow.

## The Work Out! button

The big circular button at the center of the screen is universal — it isn't tied to any one exercise. Every time you click it, all four exercises (Push, Pull, Legs, Core) each get +1 rep simultaneously, and you earn muscle for all of them at once.

## Exercises

Push, Pull, Legs, and Core are listed below the main button. Each one shows its lifetime rep total and has its own button to do a full set of just that exercise:

| Exercise | Set button |
|---|---|
| Push | Do a set (+10) |
| Pull | Do a set (+5) |
| Legs | Do a set (+12) |
| Core | Do a set (+15) |

Clicking an exercise's set button applies that many reps to that exercise only, in one click, instead of making you click the main button that many times. It's the fast way to focus on one exercise.

Pull-ups are hard, so a Pull set is small (5 reps). Core and legs movements are easier to bang out in volume, so their sets are bigger.

## Muscle

Muscle is your core stat, shown at the top of the screen along with your total reps across every exercise. Every rep — whether it comes from the main button or from doing a set — adds to it. There's no cost or purchase involved; muscle is just a running total of the work you've put in.

## Saving and coming back later

Your progress saves automatically in your browser. There's nothing to do manually — just close the tab whenever you want, and your reps and muscle will be exactly where you left them next time you open the game.

## Tips

- Use the main **Work Out!** button when you want to build all four exercises evenly.
- Use an individual exercise's set button when you want to push one category ahead of the others.
- There's no rush and no penalty for taking a break — nothing decays while you're away.
