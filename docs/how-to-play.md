# How to Play Mr. Fit

> This guide describes the current build. Mr. Fit is being reworked into a bodyweight home-workout game — this page will be rewritten once that lands.

## Quick start

1. Click the big button. That's your currency.
2. Once you have enough, buy a generator — it earns you currency automatically, even while you're not clicking.
3. Buy upgrades to make your clicks and generators stronger.
4. Come back later — you'll have earned currency while you were away.

That's the whole loop. Everything else is just more of it.

## Currency

Currency is the number at the top of the screen. You get it two ways:

- **Clicking** the big button earns you a fixed amount per click.
- **Generators** earn currency automatically, every second, based on how many you own.

## Generators

Generators are things you buy once and keep forever. Each one you own adds to your currency-per-second. The more of a generator you own, the more the *next* one costs — so it's usually worth spreading purchases across a few different generators rather than dumping everything into one.

The buy button is grayed out when you can't afford the next one yet. Keep clicking or waiting, and it'll light up.

## Upgrades

Upgrades are one-time purchases with a permanent effect — usually making your clicks worth more, or boosting a specific generator (or all of them). Once bought, an upgrade stays active for the rest of the game.

## Saving and coming back later

Your progress saves automatically in your browser — there's nothing to do manually. If you close the tab and come back, your currency and purchases will still be there.

If you're away for a while, you'll also earn some currency for the time you were gone, as if your generators kept running without you (up to a maximum amount, so leaving the game open for a week doesn't hand you infinite currency).

## Tips

- Early on, generators matter more than clicking — a couple of cheap generators quickly out-earn manual clicking.
- Upgrades that boost a generator you already own a lot of are usually worth buying immediately.
- There's no rush. This is an idle game — progress happens whether or not you're actively playing.
